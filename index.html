Deterministic

From Transformation to Artifact

Dennis turns a software change into an explicit, verifiable workflow. You decide what happens at every step.

1. Scan

Examine your project and identify the content relevant to the transformation.

2. Plan

Generate a deterministic plan describing exactly what Dennis intends to change.

3. Inspect

Review the plan before execution. Understand the transformation before it happens.

4. Verify

Validate the plan and verify its integrity before applying the transformation.

5. Apply

Execute the transformation when you are satisfied with the result.

6. Pack

Preserve the transformation as a portable DEX artifact that can be stored and shared.

The important part: Dennis does not ask you to trust an invisible script. The transformation becomes something you can inspect, verify, preserve, and reproduce.

Turn Transformations Into Artifacts

A transformation should not disappear the moment it is executed. Dennis captures it as a portable DEX artifact that can be inspected, verified, shared, stored, and preserved.

Portable

DEX artifacts are files. Store them locally, keep them in Git, share them with a team, or publish them through an artifact registry.

Inspectable

A DEX contains a human-readable transformation plan together with its metadata. You can inspect what the artifact represents without relying on a hosted service.

Verifiable

Deterministic contents can be hashed and verified. Optional cryptographic signatures can provide additional evidence of provenance and integrity.

Reproducible

Because the artifact represents a deterministic transformation, it can be preserved and reproduced independently of the machine that created it.

DEX stands for Deterministic EXchange artifact. It is designed to remain useful beyond the moment a transformation is created — as something you can keep, verify, and use again.

One Integrity Model. Many Workflows.

Dennis is built around deterministic plans, inspectable changes, and portable artifacts. Software transformation is the established foundation; document extraction is experimental, and some industry-specific workflows remain exploratory rather than finished products.

Software Developers

Plan and review code refactors, migrations, and internationalization work before applying changes. Preserve the transformation as a portable artifact that teammates can inspect and verify.

Established focus: software transformation workflows.

Structured Documents

Experimental extractors collect eligible strings from HTML, Microsoft Word DOCX, and LibreOffice ODT into structured JSON with source context. Extraction is read-only; it does not yet promise full document round-tripping.

Experimental: document extraction tools.

Legal Offices & Notaries

A potential foundation for controlled document-processing workflows where changes need to be reviewed and preserved. Domain-specific templates, compliance rules, and legal workflow integrations require further development.

Exploratory: not a legal-practice or case-management system.

Forensics & Law Enforcement

Deterministic processing plans and verifiable artifacts may support repeatable, reviewable evidence-processing procedures. Specialized forensic integrations and validated chain-of-custody workflows are not yet provided by the core.

Exploratory: integrity checks alone do not establish evidence authenticity.

Education & Research

Teach reproducible transformations, inspectable plans, and artifact-based workflows. Students and researchers can examine what a transformation intends to do instead of treating it as an opaque script.

Current focus: reproducible learning and research workflows.

Auditable Team Workflows

Share DEX artifacts that describe intended changes. Review plans before execution, verify artifact integrity, and use optional cryptographic signatures where an additional trust layer is needed.

Core capability: inspectable, portable transformation artifacts.

Clear scope, honest claims. Dennis provides a deterministic artifact foundation. Specialized industry workflows will be introduced only as their plugins and validation requirements are developed.
